## Notes
- Public GitHub repos were only used where the repo states CC0 explicitly (KayKit, Scitizia, Vuena).
- Quaternius "QAL" packs were excluded (not CC0). OpenGameArt CC BY-SA-only items were excluded. Dual-licensed items (CC BY 3.0 / CC BY-SA 3.0) are used under CC BY.
- DreamKit: only the FREE version (CC0 per its itch.io description) is included.
- Redistribution: all included licenses permit sharing the raw files; the CC BY items need their credit line kept with them.
- Original license files are in `11_Licenses/<PACK_CODE>/`.
